The instructions for any agent working in this repository are in `CLAUDE.md`: the rules that bind every change,
the verification bar before a commit or a suggestion, and how each module's dependency posture is kept honest.
`openspec/specs/` is the record of what is built and `openspec/changes/` what is planned, parked, shelved or cut.
Read `CLAUDE.md` and the specs your change touches before touching anything; `CLAUDE.md` wins where they seem to
differ.
