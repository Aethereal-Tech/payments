## Why

AgentaOS's Master Services Terms (Section 21) prohibit "standalone marketing/SEO/ads services except approved
Service Packages", and separately prohibit "offerings with no bona fide digital Product".

## What Changes

Nothing in the library. This is a consumer note, not a recommendation: a classifieds or listings operator
selling featured-listing placements or promotion products would need those products evaluated against that
clause before adopting AgentaOS as its merchant of record. It is recorded so a consumer can weigh it, not
advice about what to do with it.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None — `skip_specs: true`; this is a commercial fact for a prospective consumer, not a behaviour.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

None on the code. Relevant to any future consumer's own legal review before integrating `payments-agentaos` for
promotion-style products.
